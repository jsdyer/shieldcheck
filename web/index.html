Check your own list

Enter up to 25 domains or URLs, one per line. Your browser asks each resolver directly, using DNS over HTTPS.

What leaves your browser. Browsing captures sends nothing anywhere: a scheduled build collected them ahead of time from the URLhaus and OpenPhish feeds and the resolvers listed above. "Check your own list" sends each name you enter to every listed DNS service (CIRA, Cloudflare and Quad9) over HTTPS, and to nobody else. Nothing is stored. Some services may not answer browser requests; those cells show "!" and the scheduled captures still cover them. A block here only tells you what that DNS service returned.
